Projects

IBM Racing Challenge 2026

This project features an autonomous racing agent for the TORCS simulator, developed as a team within a student scientific club for the IBM Racing Challenge. Our agent uses reinforcement learning (the Proximal Policy Optimization algorithm) to drive autonomously, relying solely on a 70-dimensional telemetry sensor array, such as speed, angles, and track position. We implemented a custom Gym-based environment, advanced reward shaping to encourage smooth driving and optimal trajectories, and a custom synchronization pipeline that suspends the simulator process during model weight updates to prevent crashes.
